Sugar Health
Know your sugar health. Spot the signs early. Reverse it before it becomes diabetes.
🔑 What is insulin resistance?
After you eat, your blood sugar rises. Your pancreas releases insulin — a hormone that acts like a key, unlocking your cells so sugar can enter and be used as energy.

Insulin resistance means the locks have become stiff. The keys still exist, but cells stop responding. Your pancreas makes more and more insulin trying to force the doors open — until one day it can't keep up. Blood sugar stays high. That's Type 2 Diabetes.

The good news: this process takes years, is detectable early, and is reversible with the right habits.
⚠️ Warning signs to watch for
🍬 Sugar cravings 1–2h after eating
😴 Tired even after 8h sleep
🌑 Dark patches on neck or groin
⚖️ Belly fat that won't shift
🧠 Afternoon brain fog (2–4 PM)
🚽 Frequent urination at night
→ Tap Check My Risk to assess yours
🧬 What it does to your body
⚖️
Makes you gain weight — especially belly fat
High insulin keeps your body in storage mode. Fat especially builds up around the waist, which worsens the resistance further.
🌸
Disrupts hormones (PCOD link)
Excess insulin triggers the ovaries to make more testosterone — directly causing irregular periods, acne, hair loss, and PCOD.
🫀
Raises heart & blood pressure risk
Insulin resistance raises bad cholesterol (LDL, triglycerides), lowers good cholesterol, and stiffens blood vessels — silently for years.
✅ What actually reverses it
🚶Walk 10 min after meals — lowers glucose spikes by up to 30%
🥗Eat vegetables before rice/roti — fiber slows sugar absorption by 40%
🏋️Strength train 2–3×/week — muscles absorb sugar without insulin for 48h
😴Sleep 7–9 hours — one night under 6h makes cells 25% more resistant
💧Drink methi water or green tea — reduces post-meal glucose 15–20%
→ Use the Daily Habits tab to track these every day

📌 What drives T2D risk
🍬
High sugar & refined carbs
Daily sugar >30g and carb loads >250g consistently spike insulin and deplete pancreatic reserve.
😴
Poor sleep
Under 6 hours increases cortisol and growth hormone — both raise fasting blood sugar directly.
⚖️
BMI and waist size
Visceral fat around the waist produces inflammatory chemicals that block insulin receptors.
🌸
PCOD
Up to 70% of women with PCOD have insulin resistance — they have a 3–7× higher lifetime risk of T2D.
🧬
Genetics
South Asian populations (including Bangladeshis) develop T2D at a BMI 3–5 points lower than Western thresholds — and often at younger ages.
